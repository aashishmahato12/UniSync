What UniSync provides

UniSync helps you organize college-related emails, notices, dates, documents, and messages. It is an independent student tool and is not an official Herald College service. College requirements and records should be confirmed with the college directly.

Your account and connected services

You are responsible for access to your account and for the information you submit. Only connect a Gmail account you are authorized to use. You may disconnect Gmail from your UniSync profile or revoke access in your Google Account. Some features require Gmail, Google, Supabase, or AI services to be available.

Email and documents

Review recipients and content before sending messages through UniSync. A queued or sent message is not proof that a college employee received, read, or accepted it. Do not upload or send material that you do not have the right to use, or use UniSync to spam, harass, or break the law.

AI, dates, and payments

AI summaries and suggested dates may be incomplete or inaccurate. Check the original email or document before relying on a deadline, event, fee, or other important detail. A payment status you mark in UniSync is your own record; it is not a confirmation from the college or a payment processor. UniSync does not process tuition payments.

Privacy and deletion

The Privacy Policy explains what data UniSync stores and how to request account deletion. Disconnecting Gmail stops future access but does not automatically delete records already imported into UniSync.
