Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

UniSync is a student-organizing app for college notices, dates, documents, and email. It is not an official Herald College service. This policy explains what UniSync handles when you use the app.

Information you give us

We use your email address to create and sign in to your private account. If you choose, you can add profile details such as your student ID and program, upload documents, create calendar items, and write messages to college addresses. Your theme, notice-read marks, and self-marked payment status may be saved in your browser.

When you connect Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. UniSync requests permission to read your Gmail and send email on your behalf. The Google permission to read mail is broad, but UniSync's import workflow searches for recent messages from @heraldcollege.edu.np. It stores the selected messages' sender, subject, date, body, AI-generated summary, and extracted event details in your private account. Supported PDF, JPEG, and PNG attachments from those messages may also be stored. UniSync does not need to import your entire mailbox to show college notices.

When you choose to send a college email in UniSync, the app queues your message and uses your connected Gmail account to send it to a @heraldcollege.edu.np address. UniSync stores the message and delivery status. We do not use Gmail content for advertising or sell it.

AI processing

To create notice summaries and event suggestions, UniSync sends the selected college email's subject, sender, body, date, and attachment names to its n8n automation workflow, which uses Google Gemini. When you ask the AI assistant a question, UniSync sends your question and relevant saved notice, event, or extracted document text to that workflow. AI answers can be wrong; check important dates and fees against the original college message.

Important: UniSync's current Gemini API setup may use an unpaid plan. Under Google's unpaid Gemini API terms, Google may use submitted content and responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may inspect them. Do not connect a mailbox or submit information you are uncomfortable processing this way.

Where information is processed

UniSync uses Supabase for accounts, private records, and file storage; Vercel to host the app and its server functions; and an n8n workflow with Google Gemini for AI features. Google processes Gmail connection and delivery requests. Those services receive only the information needed for the features described above and operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Storage, access, and deletion

Account records, imported college notices, events, attachments, uploaded documents, and queued emails are kept so you can use them in UniSync. Connected Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted before storage. Your signed-in account controls access to its records. Disconnecting Gmail removes UniSync's stored connection token and stops future syncing, but it does not automatically erase notices, events, files, or messages already saved in your account.

To request deletion of your UniSync account and saved data, email mahatoaashish5@gmail.com from the email address on your account. You can also revoke UniSync's Gmail access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops new Gmail retrieval but does not by itself remove previously saved UniSync records.

Security

UniSync uses authenticated accounts, private data access rules, HTTPS connections, and encrypted Gmail connection tokens. No online system can promise absolute security. If you believe your account or data is at risk, contact us at the address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this page when UniSync changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact mahatoaashish5@gmail.com.
